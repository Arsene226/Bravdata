"Free" is a word that invites a second question: what is the catch. This page answers it in the open, because a check whose scope is vague is not worth taking.

## What it is

A machine read of what the public web already says about your business. You give it your website address or a public social page, and your business name. It reads that page and the public signals it can reach from it, and nothing behind a password.

What comes back is short and specific:

- **A BravData Visibility Score from 0 to 100.** It is our own calculation, built from up to seven parts, and it prints the method version beside it so you can see which version produced the number.
- **The findings behind the score.** Each one names the source we read and the date we read it. Three findings is the cap; the evidence behind each one is not trimmed to fit.
- **The parts we could not check**, labelled as not checked rather than scored. A source we cannot read is never recorded as a zero.
- **What to change first**, in the order that matters.

It takes seconds, and it runs without you creating an account.

## What it is not

- It is **not a consultation**. No person reviews the result before you see it. It is a machine reading public information, and it says so.
- It is **not an audit of your business**. It cannot see your books, your phone logs, your bookings or your customers.
- It is **not a ranking check**. Nobody outside a search engine can read a search position off the public web, so we do not print one.
- It is **not a promise of more customers**. It describes how findable and how clear your public presence is, and it stops there.
- It is **not a form you fill in before you get the answer**. The result is shown to you first. Saving it is a separate, optional step.

## What it asks of you

Nothing you would not give a stranger who offered to look at your shop window: the address of a public page, your business name, and a few seconds.

If you choose to save your results, the form asks for a name and an email address, and a phone number if you want to give one. That is stored with the report you just saw, and it is used to send you the report you asked for. It is not a subscription to anything and it does not enrol you in a plan.

## Why it is free

Because it is the same engine a paid plan uses, run once, on public information only. It is the cheapest honest way for someone who has never heard of us to decide whether we understand what we are looking at — without a call, a card or a calendar link.

There is no hidden obligation at the end of it. The 30/60/90-day plan, the content calendar and the publishing side belong to a paid plan. The check, the score, the findings and the first priorities do not.

## Where it stops

The free check reads one public page and the signals around it. It cannot connect to your listings, your analytics or your accounts; that needs an account and your permission, and it is what turns a "not checked" line into an observation we can stand behind.

## What this page cannot tell you

- Whether the check will find anything wrong with your business. Some public presences are in good order, and a check that always found three problems would be a sales tool rather than a measurement.
- Whether fixing what it finds will change anything. We do not claim cause.
- Whether we are the right people to do the fixing. That is your judgement, and the free check is the cheapest way to start forming it.
