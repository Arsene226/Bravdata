## What a visitor sees

LocalRealtorAI describes itself as "Real estate intelligence for San Antonio." Its site names Esther Tonde, REALTOR® · Real Broker, LLC, in San Antonio, Texas, and leads with "Find a home that fits your life."

The site pairs plain-English guides about buying and owning in San Antonio with calculators, and it states its own numbers on its homepage: 49 published articles, 6 topics, and 5 live tools — the Buyer Offer Planner, two "Matcher" tools (Find My Ideal Home and Find My Ideal Location), a Mortgage & Affordability Calculator, and a Rent or Buy calculator.

## What stands out

Three things on the homepage are worth pointing at, and all three are the site's own statements rather than our judgment:

- **Sources are carried with the claims.** The site states that every article carries the sources behind it, and that an article moves to published only when each factual claim has a dated source row behind it.
- **Calculators show their assumptions.** Each tool is described as computing from the figures a visitor enters — "arithmetic, not a guess" — and showing the assumptions it used.
- **Absence is said plainly.** The homepage includes this line: "When we have no verified figure, the page says so instead of guessing one."

Those are the same habits BravData holds its own reports to: named sources, dated evidence, and a plain "we could not verify this" instead of a made-up number. A local business that states those habits publicly is worth a spotlight.

The site also posts a clear disclaimer: educational information only, not financial, legal or tax advice, and not a loan offer, pre-approval or guarantee of any rate or price.
