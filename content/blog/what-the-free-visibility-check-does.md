This is BravData's own description of the free check we actually run today. It claims no client result, no ranking and no statistic.

## What the check does

Give it the address of your website or one of your public social pages, and your business name. The check reads what is publicly available about your business: the page you gave us, the signals it can reach from there, and nothing behind a password. It takes a few seconds, and it is machine-run — no person reviews it before you do. There is no card, no login and no call to see the result.

## What comes back

- **A BravData Visibility Score from 0 to 100.** It is our own calculation, not a figure handed over by Google, Meta or an AI vendor. It is built from up to seven parts — Website Health, Content Authority, Search Presence, Local Presence, Reputation, Authority Signals and AI Readiness — and each part opens to show the checks that produced it. The method version is printed beside the score.
- **The findings behind it.** Each one names the source we read and the date we read it, so you can check it yourself. Three findings is the cap; evidence is never trimmed to make the list shorter.
- **What we could not check**, labelled as not checked rather than scored. A source we cannot read is never recorded as a zero.
- **What to change first**, in the order that matters.

## Three things the score is not

- It is **not a search ranking**. Nobody outside a search engine can read a search position off the public web, so we do not print one.
- It is **not a promise of more customers**. It describes how findable and how clear your public presence is, and it stops there.
- It is **not a guess dressed as data**. Where a part of the score cannot be evidenced from public sources, it is left out and said so — missing data never becomes a zero.

## Where the free check stops

The check, the score, the findings and the first priorities are free. The 30/60/90-day plan, the content calendar and social publishing belong to a paid plan, and connecting an account is what turns a "not checked" line into an observation we can stand behind. Nothing about the free check obliges you to buy anything.

## What is live today

The paragraph above describes what is live on this site as of 11 October 2026: the credential-free scan engine, the BravData Visibility Score with its published method version, and the report behind it. We do not describe anything as working until it does — where a source is not connected, the report says so instead of showing a number for it.
